# AI Visual Prompt Pack v2 — Shikkha24 cover images (Nano Banana 2.1 × Google Flow, 2026-10-16)

**Platform:** Google Flow — flow.google.com (Google's AI creative studio; images come from the **Nano Banana 2.1** model, released 2026-10-06 — the current workhorse: Flash-class speed, 1K/2K/4K output, strong multi-turn editing, invisible SynthID watermark on every output).

## How to use in Flow (60 seconds per cover)

1. Open **flow.google.com** → New project → choose the **image** asset type → model **Nano Banana 2.1** (not Lite — Lite skips the multi-reference consistency we rely on; not Pro — 2.1 is the best quality/price fit).
2. Set the **output size** to 2K and **aspect ratio 16:9** (Flow downsizes gracefully to our 1200×630 OG crop; 4K is wasted on web).
3. Paste ONE prompt block from below (each is a self-contained paragraph — Nano Banana reads narrative prompts best, not keyword lists).
4. If the result is close but imperfect, **stay in the same chat and reply conversationally** — NB 2.1's multi-turn editing is its strength ("make the background lighter", "remove the extra desks"). Each follow-up regenerates with everything else held consistent.
5. Download → CMS **Media** upload → attach as featured image.

## House rules (non-negotiable)

1. **NO PEOPLE in any image** — no human faces, no photorealistic humans, no silhouettes with visible faces. Scenes are built from objects, spaces, and symbolic props only (see the style block's closing line).
2. **NO PHOTO-REALISM** — every cover is flat editorial illustration (our house style below). Real events use real photos from contributors; AI covers are illustrations, always distinguishable as such.
3. **No text or letters inside images** — Nano Banana renders Latin text well but Bengali script poorly and we never ship pixel text in a news brand. Headlines live in HTML.
4. Every AI image gets credit `চিত্র: AI` + Bengali alt text in the CMS.
5. Outputs carry an invisible SynthID watermark (Google's standard) — no action needed, do not try to strip it.

## The house style block (inside every prompt)

Every prompt below ends with the same three sentences — keep them verbatim for series consistency:

```text
… Style: flat editorial illustration, clean vector shapes, minimal paper-grain texture, warm blue (#1D4ED8) and red (#DC2626) accents on a soft off-white (#FAFAF7) background, gentle ambient lighting, no gradients heavier than a subtle wash. Absolutely no people, no human faces, no hands, no photorealistic elements. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermarks.
```

**Format for follow-up edits:** `Same scene, but <one change>.` (e.g. "Same scene, but it is night and the windows glow warm.")

## Sizing

- Featured/cover: 16:9, generate at 2K in Flow, downscale/crop to ≥1200×630 for the CMS (the site's OG card size).
- Article body images: 3:2 or 16:9, ≥1000px wide.
- Poll/quiz accents: 1:1, 800×800 (ask Flow for "square 1:1" in the same chat).

## Why these settings (Nano Banana 2.1 notes)

- Narrative paragraphs beat tag lists — the model reads scene → composition → style order.
- The identical closing style block across prompts is what makes 12 covers look like ONE brand.
- Multi-turn follow-ups ("Same scene, but …") preserve composition better than re-rolling.
- 2K generation keeps text-free vector art crisp at web sizes; 4K only for print-scale needs.
- Every output carries an invisible SynthID watermark — expected, fine for our use.

## Usage policy recap

AI images are **illustrations** for evergreen/routine/scholarship/circular covers. Real news events, people and campuses = real contributed/sourced photos, credited to their photographer. Credit AI covers `চিত্র: AI` in the CMS credit field, always.
