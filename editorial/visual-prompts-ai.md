# AI Visual Prompt Pack — Shikkha24 cover images (v1, 2026-10-16)

**How to use:** paste a prompt into any image generator (Bing Image Creator — free, or Midjourney/DALL·E/Leonardo). Generate, download the 16:9 result, crop to 1200×630 if the tool allows, then upload in CMS **Media** → attach as the article's featured image. Alt text in Bengali, credit line: "চিত্র: AI" — every AI image carries that credit (news honesty: AI images are illustrations, never presented as photographs).

**Fixed style block** (keep identical in every prompt — this is our series look):

```text
flat editorial illustration, clean vector style, warm blue and red accent palette on a white background, soft geometric shapes, courageous negative space, no text, no letters, no words, no watermark, high detail, 16:9 wide banner
```

**Negative / avoid list** (for tools with a negative field): `text, letters, words, watermark, photorealistic faces, brand logos, cluttered composition, dark muddy colors`

**Reusable formula:** `[SCENE] + fixed style block + aspect-ratio flag`

- Midjourney: append ` --ar 16:9 --style raw`
- DALL·E / Bing / Leonardo: ask "wide 16:9" in the sentence (the style block already says it)

## Sizing

- Featured/cover: 16:9, target ≥1200×630 (fits the OG card).
- Article body images: 3:2 or 16:9, ≥1000px wide.
- Poll/quiz accents: square 1:1, 800×800.

## House rules (summary)

1. AI images = **illustrations only**; real events, people and campuses use real (contributed/sourced) photos.
2. Every AI image gets credit "চিত্র: AI" in the CMS credit field.
3. No text/letters inside the image — headlines live in HTML, never in pixels (garbled Bengali kills polish).
4. Same style block every time = one recognizable visual identity.
