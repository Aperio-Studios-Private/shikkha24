## Design decision — a layout, not a new collection

`Articles.layout = 'photoStory'` rather than a `PhotoStories` collection. A separate
collection would **split SEO authority** and duplicate the editorial workflow
(draft → review → publish, versioning, scheduling) for what is a *presentation* of
an article.

## Planned deliverables

| Piece | Notes |
|---|---|
| `Articles.layout` | gains `photoStory` |
| Photo-story block array | image, caption, credit, `fullBleed` |
| Template | Full-bleed images, captions beneath, scroll-linked |

⚠️ **Zero CLS is mandatory.** Every image must reserve its box via `aspect-ratio`
from the media record's **true dimensions** — CLS < 0.02 is a hard gate, and this
is the most image-dense page on the site.

| Piece | Notes |
|---|---|
| `ImageGallery` JSON-LD | emitted when `layout === 'photoStory'` |
| `Articles.videoUrl` + `videoProvider` | an article with a video renders a click-to-load player **above** the body |
| `/video` hub | lists every video-bearing article, reusing the existing listing + pagination components |

⚠️ **`/video` is 1 segment ⇒ strict CSP ⇒ the player would be blocked.** It must be
tagged with the `SHIKKHA24_CSP_MEDIA` profile introduced by **S15**, or it must link
out instead of embedding. This is why S17 depends on S15.
