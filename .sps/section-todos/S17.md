# S17 — Photo Story & Video News

**Status:** ✅ **BUILT 2026-09-28** · **Requested as:** the missing half of feature #10
**Specification:** `docs/feature-batch-2.md` §6 · **Index:** `docs/feature-register.md`

⚠️ **Photo Gallery is already built** (S9). Only photo story and video news were outstanding.

## Design decision — a layout, not a new collection

`Articles.layout = 'photoStory'` rather than a `PhotoStories` collection. A separate collection
would **split SEO authority** and duplicate the editorial workflow (draft → review → publish,
versioning, scheduling) for what is a *presentation* of an article.

The photo story **does not replace the prose** — it renders the frames ahead of it. A layout that
silently discarded what an editor wrote would be a surprising thing for a CMS to do.

## What was built

### 1. `Articles` gains four fields

`layout` (`standard` | `photoStory`, indexed) · `photoStory` (frame array: image, caption, credit,
`fullBleed`) · `videoProvider` (youtube | facebook) · `videoUrl`.

`photoStory` and `videoProvider` are conditional on the fields that make them meaningful, so an
ordinary article does not carry a permanently empty frame list an editor has to scroll past.

### 2. `PhotoStory.astro` + `lib/photo-story.ts`

⚠️ **Zero CLS is the hard gate, and this is the most image-dense page on the site.** Every frame
reserves its box from the media record's **true dimensions** — `resolveCmsImage` supplies real
`width`/`height`, which are written onto the `<img>` *and* into an `aspect-ratio`. Measured on the
fixture: `--frame-ratio: 1600 / 1067` on all four frames.

**An image whose dimensions cannot be measured is skipped, not guessed.** A guessed ratio is a
layout shift with extra steps.

The resolution lives in `lib/photo-story.ts` rather than inside the component because the page
needs the same list **twice** — once to render and once to build the `ImageGallery` JSON-LD.
Resolving independently in each place is how structured data ends up describing images the page
does not show.

**Scroll-linked with no JavaScript**: CSS scroll-driven animation behind `@supports
(animation-timeline: view())` and `prefers-reduced-motion`, the same shape as the S8 scrollytelling
block. A browser without support, or a reader who asked for less motion, gets plain stacked frames
— and the frames are fully readable with JavaScript off, which matters because they *are* the
content.

### 3. `ImageGallery` JSON-LD

Emitted **in addition to** `NewsArticle`, never instead of it — the article is still the page's
subject and the gallery only describes its presentation. `Layout` already accepts an array.

### 4. Video news — `lib/video.ts`, `MediaEmbed` above the body, `/video` hub

`videoEmbedProps()` is the single place an article's stored `videoUrl` becomes player props, and it
is shared by the article page and the hub. For YouTube the URL is parsed down to the 11-character
id; for Facebook it must be an `https` URL on one of Meta's hosts. **The output is never the
input** — `MediaEmbed` assembles the frame URL from a fixed template.

⚠️ **The `/video` hub features the newest video with a player**, and the rest are cards. The spec
calls it "a hub listing every article that carries a video" *and* decides it must be tagged
`SHIKKHA24_CSP_MEDIA` because it is "the same *embeds third-party video* category as `/live-tv`".
Those two only agree if the page embeds something — a page of plain cards needs no `frame-src` at
all, and tagging it `MEDIA` would be a relaxation nothing uses.

⚠️ **`/video/page/:n` is tagged too.** It is 3 segments, and the article rule excludes only a
`page/` in the *middle* position — so without its own rule, page 2 of the hub would be served the
strict policy and its player would die silently.

### 5. 🚨 The defect `verify:apache` found

**`Articles.videoProvider` can be `facebook`, but the article profile's `frame-src` named only
YouTube.** An article carrying a Facebook video would therefore have been **silently dead on the
highest-traffic route**: HTTP 200, valid markup, a valid policy — and the iframe blocked with no
error anywhere. The exact failure class the third profile was introduced to prevent on `/live-tv`,
sitting unnoticed on every article.

It surfaced only because S17 made an *article* render a `MediaEmbed` for the first time, which is
what made the harness's expectation mismatch.

**Two changes, and the second is the one that matters:**

1. The article profile gained the Facebook hosts in `frame-src`.
2. The harness became **provider-aware**: it reads `data-media-provider` off each page — the
   component's own statement of which provider the reader will frame — and asserts the served
   policy allows **that** provider. A blanket "does the policy mention Facebook" would not do;
   what matters is whether the policy permits the frame the page actually creates.

⚠️ **Consequence: the article and media profiles now carry the same policy.** Both env vars remain,
because they dispatch by different route families, so a later change to one cannot silently widen
the other. The static half of the proof is asserted by `verify:build-output`; the runtime half by
`verify:apache`. **Neither harness alone proves the dispatch any more.**

- **The `/video` tag is load-bearing.** Stripped from both `.htaccess` copies →
  `verify:apache`: `/video/ is served a video-capable policy — HTTP 200, got strict`, plus
  `/video → strict, but the page needs relaxed-video`. `verify:build-output`:
  `.htaccess tags /video for the media profile` and `SHIKKHA24_CSP_MEDIA is scoped to exactly 2
  routes — 1 tag(s)`.
- **The article profile's Facebook hosts are load-bearing.** Stripped →
  `verify:apache`: `/exams-results/15/demo-board-challenge-result → frames a Facebook video, but
  the policy forbids the frame`. `verify:build-output`: `the article profile frames Facebook`.
  Restored, re-verified in sync, all green.
- **Both checks are non-vacuous** because the fixtures cover **both providers** — one article with
  a YouTube video and one with a Facebook video. With only a YouTube fixture the provider-aware
  check could never fire, and the Facebook half of `MediaEmbed` would never render anywhere in the
  build. That is precisely how the defect survived.
- **The no-request invariant holds**: the Facebook video article renders `0` iframes and the source
  URL only as a data attribute.

## Deviations & open items

- ⚠️ **`/video` has no ad slot.** The spec did not call for one, and adding a position to
  `AD_POSITIONS` is a contract change (`liveTvTop` was added at S15 because the spec asked for it).
  Its profile therefore includes ad domains it does not use — a hygiene note, not a failure, and
  the same route-family over-relaxation `/quiz` already has.
- ⚠️ **The article and media CSP profiles are now byte-identical.** They are kept separate rather
  than merged so that a future change to one cannot silently widen the other; the reasoning is in
  `docs/security.md` §13.9 and in `.htaccess` itself.
- ⚠️ **`Articles.layout` is `index: true` but nothing filters on it yet.** The index is there
  because a future `/photo-stories` listing would want it; if that listing is never built, the
  index is a small write cost for nothing. Noted rather than removed, since the field is the
  feature's entry point.
- **CLS was verified by construction, not by a Lighthouse run.** The reserved boxes are asserted in
  the built HTML; the CLS < 0.02 measurement itself is a page-level Lighthouse gate that needs a
  production deployment (S12).
- The `verify:search-init` SKIP and the S13 `/admin/reporter` manual check remain outstanding and
  are unchanged by this section.
