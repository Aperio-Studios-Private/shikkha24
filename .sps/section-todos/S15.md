## Owner decisions that constrain this section

| Decision | Answer | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Video source | **Both YouTube and Facebook** — the CMS stores the provider, the page picks the player | 2026-09-18 |

⚠️ **Self-hosted streaming is impossible.** It needs a VPS with RTMP/nginx, which
violates the zero-recurring-cost constraint. An embedded player is the only
feasible path.

## 🚨 The architectural change this section forces

`/live-tv` must embed YouTube **and** Facebook iframes. Neither existing CSP
profile fits:

| Profile | `frame-src` | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Strict | `'none'` | The iframe is blocked |
| `SHIKKHA24_CSP_RELAXED` | ad domains only | **No video** — silently blocked |
| `SHIKKHA24_CSP_ARTICLE` | YouTube + ads | **No Facebook**; also semantic drift to use an `_ARTICLE` variable for a non-article route |

Widening `RELAXED` was **rejected as a security regression** — the homepage, every
category listing and `/quiz` would gain iframe permission they do not need.

**Decision: a third profile, `SHIKKHA24_CSP_MEDIA`, scoped to `/live-tv`.**

⚠️ Adding it breaks `scripts/verify-apache.mjs`, whose expectation logic (corrected
in M16 to key on `data-ad-slot`) assumes **exactly two** profiles. These must land
in the **same commit**:

1. teach `verify-apache.mjs` the third profile
2. document it in `docs/security.md` §5.1
3. assert the `/live-tv` rule in `verify-build-output.mjs`, **counter-proved** by
   injecting its removal
